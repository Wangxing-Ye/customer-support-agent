# Sample knowledge base content for RAGFlow (professional services)

## About Summit Advisory Group
Summit Advisory Group provides consulting and advisory services for small businesses in the United States. We help with strategy, process improvement, and project scoping. We do not provide formal legal, tax, or medical advice through this chat assistant.

## Services and pricing
- Introductory Consultation (slug: intro-consult) — Free, 30 minutes, bookable online (Zoom). Pay-when: none (confirmed immediately). There is no one-booking-per-email limit.
- Strategy Session (slug: strategy-session) — USD 500 per hour. Each Strategy Session is one hour. Bookable online (Zoom). Pay-when: checkout to hold. Booking holds the slot for 15 minutes with a demo checkout URL; it is not confirmed until payment. After you say you have paid, the assistant confirms and emails a cancellation code. This demo does not use Stripe.
- Document Review (slug: document-review) — USD 250 per hour, with detailed email feedback. Not bookable online; request via a support ticket.

## Booking policy
Appointments are available Monday–Friday, America/Los_Angeles, on the hour only:
9:00 AM, 10:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 1:00 PM, 2:00 PM, 3:00 PM, 4:00 PM, 5:00 PM
(no noon and no half-hour starts). Booking requires your name and email.
Free intro consults are confirmed immediately by email (appointment ID, cancellation code, Zoom link). Strategy Sessions stay held until the demo checkout is completed; unpaid holds expire after 15 minutes and the slot is released. Do not treat a hold as a confirmed appointment.

## Cancellation policy
You may cancel online by providing the email used at booking and the cancellation code from your confirmation email. Self-service cancellation is not available within 24 hours of the appointment start time; contact us for a human to help.

## Escalation
If you need a licensed professional opinion, have a billing dispute, or ask to speak with a person, we create a support ticket. We need your name, question or request description, email, phone number, and a convenient time to call. Most follow-up is by email; we call when a conversation would be more effective. A team member replies within our stated SLA (shown when the ticket is created).
